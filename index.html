Yes — for a rebuild of up to 5 pages, that's the full price: half to book your slot, half when the site launches. If your project needs more (extra pages, online store, custom features), we flag it in the free audit and quote it separately before any work starts. You'll never get a surprise bill.

What does the free audit include?

We score your current site on speed, mobile experience, design, and conversion — and send you a short, plain-English breakdown of what's hurting you and what we'd fix first. No obligation: if the audit says your site is fine, we'll tell you.

Can you really finish in 7 days?

Yes, because the scope is fixed and the process is set. The clock starts once we have your content and your Day 1 approval. If a delay is on our end, we keep working at no extra cost until you're live.

What do you need from me?

Your logo and photos if you have them, access to your current site/domain, and quick feedback at two checkpoints. No photos or copy? We can work with what exists and clean up the words for you — that's part of the rebuild.

Do I own my website?

Completely. Your domain, your content, your logins. We build it so any developer could pick it up later — including you. No monthly lock-in required to keep your own site online.

What if I don't have a website yet?

Then it's not a rebuild, it's a build — same 7-day process and same flat approach for a simple 5-page site. Request the audit and tell us you're starting from scratch.
